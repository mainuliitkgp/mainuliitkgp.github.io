### About me

Hi, I am a third year PhD student at the [CopeNLU](https://www.copenlu.com/) group, Department of Computer Science, [University of Copenhagen](https://www.ku.dk/english/). I am also affiliated with the [Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence](https://www.aicentre.dk/). I work with my PhD supervisors [Isabelle Augenstein](https://isabelleaugenstein.github.io/) and [Pepa Atanasova](https://apepa.github.io/) on **Interpreting how LLMs encode factual and personalization concepts**.
