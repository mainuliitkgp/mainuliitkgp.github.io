### About me

Hi, I am a third-year PhD student at the [CopeNLU](https://www.copenlu.com/) group, Department of Computer Science, [University of Copenhagen](https://www.ku.dk/english/). I am also affiliated with the [Pioneer Centre for Artificial Intelligence](https://www.aicentre.dk/). I work with my PhD supervisors [Prof. Isabelle Augenstein](https://isabelleaugenstein.github.io/) and [Prof. Pepa Atanasova](https://apepa.github.io/) on **Interpreting how LLMs encode factual and personalization concepts**. 

My research interest in Mechanistic Interpretability focuses on how LLMs encode different knowledge interactions into their model space and how different components conceptually associate with their biased behaviours. Recently, I started working on understanding how Mixture-of-Experts LLMs encode personalized concepts into distinct routing paths, offering better interpretability than dense LLMs.
